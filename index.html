Pathfinder

Connect sequences in alternating order as fast as you can.

~4 minutes

What this measures

This test is adapted from the Trail Making Test, a well-established neuropsychological assessment. It targets two core cognitive abilities:

1
Processing Speed How quickly you take in information and respond
2
Cognitive Flexibility Your ability to switch between different rules and tasks

Processing Speed

Processing speed is how quickly you take in information, make a decision, and respond. It's one of the strongest predictors of overall cognitive performance as you age.

Research shows measurable declines in processing speed beginning as early as your mid-40s — and it's one of the first cognitive abilities to change with age.

Hoyer et al., 2004; Salthouse, 2000

In daily life, it shows up in moments like keeping up with a fast conversation, reacting while driving, or following instructions in real time.

Cognitive Flexibility

Cognitive flexibility is your ability to shift between different rules, tasks, or ways of thinking. It's what lets you adapt when plans change or switch gears between competing demands.

Performance on cognitive flexibility tasks has been shown to predict independence in everyday activities like managing medications, cooking, and shopping — and is used clinically to screen for driving fitness.

Cahn-Weiner et al., 2002; American Medical Association

When flexibility slows down, you may notice it's harder to multitask, adjust to unexpected changes, or shift between topics in conversation.

Test instructions

To start, circles with numbers and letters will appear on screen
Tap them in alternating order: 1 → A → 2 → B → 3 → C…
Go as fast as you can while staying accurate — your time directly affects your score
Wrong taps count as errors but won't stop the clock
There are 3 rounds — each one changes the rule
