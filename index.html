Pathfinder

A cognitive assessment of mental flexibility and processing speed

What this measures

Mental flexibility Your ability to switch between different rules
Processing speed How quickly you can scan and identify targets
Working memory Keeping track of where you are in the sequence

This test is adapted from the Trail Making Test, a well-established neuropsychological assessment used in clinical and research settings.
